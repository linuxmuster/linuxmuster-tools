migrate()[source]

Migrate a legacy sophomorix-group entry (living in OU=Projects) to OU=LMNGroups, converting its sophomorixType to lmngroup.

A move only changes the entry’s DN, not its attributes: the member list is untouched, and memberOf on the members is a computed backlink that follows automatically.
